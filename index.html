Who we are

A league built by drivers, for drivers.

Speediots Racing is an online sim racing league and esports community that brings drivers together for competitive championships, organised race nights and community events. Our home sim is Assetto Corsa Competizione, and our grids span everything from GT4 rookies to GT3 aliens.

The name is a wink — we take ourselves lightly and our racing seriously. Every event runs on published regulations, with stewarding and penalties handled consistently so that the fight on track stays hard but fair.

Registration, entry lists and results live on The Sim Grid; the paddock chat, briefings and banter live on our Discord.
