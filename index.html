The league

Built by drivers,
run like a series.

Speediots Racing is an online sim racing league and esports community where passion for racing meets the thrill of competition.

Our home sim is Assetto Corsa Competizione, and our grids run from GT4 rookies to GT3 front-runners. The name is a wink — we take ourselves lightly and the racing seriously.

Every event runs on published regulations. Incidents are reviewed by stewards, decisions are applied consistently, and the fight on track stays hard but fair.

Clear regulations

Conduct, stewarding and penalty rules published up front — identical for every driver.

Consistent stewarding

Incidents reviewed after the race. Decisions explained. No favourites.

Live broadcasts

Championship rounds streamed with commentary on Twitch, replays on YouTube.

Community first

All levels welcome. Rookies get help, fast drivers get a real fight.
